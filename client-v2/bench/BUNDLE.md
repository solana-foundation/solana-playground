# Bundle sizes

Generated by `yarn bundle:info` from the production build. Do not edit it by hand: `yarn test-build` fails unless a fresh measurement reproduces it byte for byte. To refresh it, rebase onto the base branch, run `REACT_APP_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID= yarn build-fast`, then `yarn bundle:info`, and commit the refreshed file.

`*.js` counts `static/js/*.js` and the Monaco workers at the root of `build/`. The `*.module.wasm` files are not measured. `main`, any other named chunk, and each worker (`worker:<name>`) get a row of their own; `other chunks` sums the rest.

Sizes are rounded: to 10 B under 1 kB, up to the next 10 kB under 1000 kB, and to 0.01 MB above. A kB is 1024 B and an MB is 1024 kB. A fresh cell within 11 KiB of the committed cell (`DISPLAY_STEP_TOLERANCE_BYTES`) keeps the committed value, so a rebuild of the same commit leaves this file unchanged.
