# Tutorial Frame Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans
> (inline) to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`)
> syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A tutorial with a definition opens in its own frame: the navigation
folded to its rail, the chat column with its head, and the workspace slot
filling the rest. Every later tutorial ticket mounts into it.

**Architecture:** The frame is a mode of the existing layout shell, not a
second shell. `BaseLayoutShell` takes a `tutorial` prop. With it, on the work
screen from `LAYOUT_BREAKPOINTS.phone` up, the work area is laid out as the
chat column, then the stage over the console. The stage and the console are the
shell's outlets, so the editor and the terminal never remount on the way in or
out. The navigation's open state comes from the frame's fold rules while the
frame shows, and from the saved layout otherwise, so leaving restores it with
no write. The fold rules, the width clamp, the head, the dialogs, the pin and
the hint live in a new widget, `widgets/tutorial-frame`. `views/flow/Flow.tsx`
composes it with the shell, since one widget may not import another.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, Tailwind 4, the design system's `dropdown-menu`,
`dialog`, `tooltip` and `kbd` from `shared/ui`, vitest in-source blocks and
Testing Library.

**Spec:** `openspec/changes/tutorial-flow-v2/specs/tutorial-workspace/spec.md`,
"Rail, chat, workspace, in that order", "The navigation folds to its rail in a
tutorial" and the list of "Keyboard paths are listed" (the keys themselves are
their tickets').

## Sources, and where they differ

The prototype (`almaz/mobile-tutorial-prototype`,
`design-system/src/prototype/tutorial-v2/desktop`, `Column.tsx`, `Nav.tsx`,
`NavHint.tsx`, `ChatColumn.tsx`, `keys.ts`) wins over `SPEC.md` and the spec
delta (prototype over spec, 23 Oct):

- **Default width under 1280:** the prototype gives way faster under 1280,
  `400 − (1280 − W) × 0.3125`, so 1152 gets 360, not the delta's 383. Taken
  from the prototype.
- **The hint:** the prototype's line carries no ⌘B; the delta's does. Taken
  from the prototype.
- **Arrow keys on the handle** (16 px, 64 with Shift, Home and End): in the
  prototype, not the delta. Taken.

Decided here, no source settles it (sent to Tool - Design alignment):

- **Below a wide window** (768 to 1023) no source draws the frame, so a
  tutorial keeps today's layout there; the phone keeps its pager until 5.1.
- **The files column** stays beside the stage, under no stage bar and held
  open (no Hide, ⌘E does nothing, since nothing there could reopen it), until
  the workspace's tree (2.2) replaces it: it holds the lesson's steps and the
  files a later step opens. Found by CI: without it, eight lesson specs and
  the learner lost Steps, Skip and every file but the open one.
- **Start over…** is in the menu and asks first, in the prototype's words.
  The engine (task 3.1) has landed, but its `init` and ports are the feed's
  (3.3), so the item stays disabled until 3.3 passes the handler.
- **Before Start** a definition tutorial keeps its About page in today's
  layout; the frame opens once it is started, until the intro card (3.3)
  replaces the About page.
- **The chat body** holds the assistant's own conversation and composer until
  the feed lands (task 3.3).

## Global Constraints

- Rail 52 px (`RAIL_WIDTH`). Chat default `chatDefaultWidth(W)`: 360 at 1152,
  400 at 1280, 421 at 1440, never under 360 or over 480. Range 340 to 560, and
  never so wide that the workspace drops under 560. A double click resets it.
- The chat head is 44 px, with "Tutorials" back, the tutorial's name and a ⋯
  menu, `aria-label="More"`, holding "Start over…" and "Keyboard shortcuts".
- The navigation folds on entry, whatever the saved layout holds. ⌘B or the
  rail's "Expand navigation" opens it as an overlay over the chat with a scrim.
  A pick, the scrim, Esc or ⌘B closes it and focus goes back. The pin, labelled
  "Keep open" and "Fold while I learn", docks it until the learner leaves.
- The hint "The menu is folded to icons while you learn" shows on the first
  fold only, for 6 s or until a pointer down or a key.
- The frame shows on the work screen of a tutorial route whose tutorial has a
  `definition`. Under a page (Tutorials, Programs) or Home it does not, and the
  navigation follows the saved layout.
- Events, prefix `tutorial_frame`: `tutorial_frame_menu_opened` (`via`: `key`
  or `button`), `tutorial_frame_menu_pinned` (`pinned`),
  `tutorial_frame_chat_resized` (`width`, `via`: `drag`, `key` or `reset`).
