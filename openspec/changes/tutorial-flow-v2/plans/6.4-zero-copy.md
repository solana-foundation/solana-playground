## Spike (6 Nov)

Checked against Playground's build server (api.solpg.io), a local SBF runtime
(LiteSVM 0.1.0) and devnet's `getMinimumBalanceForRentExemption`.

| Script says | Measured |
| --- | --- |
| The unboxed board fails the build; `Box` fixes it | Both build: the stack overflow is a warning, and `improveOutput` strips it. `init_board` (12 cells) runs unboxed. From 16 cells, the call fails boxed and unboxed (`Board::try_deserialize` overflows itself). No size fails unboxed and passes boxed |
| The map runs out of heap at 40 KB | At 30 KB: the 20 KB copy plus `resize`'s 30 KB buffer. Log: `memory allocation failed, out of memory` |
| A grow of more than 10 KB is refused | Yes: `AccountReallocExceedsLimit` (3016), no "out of memory" in its log |
| `init_big_map` makes 10 KB without `load_init` | Yes |
| `#[account(zero_copy)]` on `[u8; 40952]` | Does not compile (bytemuck `Pod`). `zero_copy(unsafe)` + `#[repr(C)]` does, as upstream's tutorial |
| Paint at 35,000 after Grow to 40 KB | `Painted 2 bytes at 35000` |
| (not in the script) `Grow`, `GrowBig`, `Paint` check the map's `seeds` | Another signer's grow or paint fails `ConstraintSeeds` (2006); the owner's numbers above hold (rerun on the final program) |
| 10 KB of rent is about 0.072 SOL | 0.0527 SOL on devnet and mainnet; 40 KB is 0.2087 SOL |

Program: 278 KB. The deploy and each upgrade go through the flow's airdrop
top-up (`fundForDeploy`) and program extension (`real-deploy.ts`).

## Shape

Four steps, the board and its planned failure dropped (design gap, decided):

1. `create`: `InitMap` (question: 10 KB), `Map`, `init_map`; build.
2. `deploy`: deploy; who pays rent (prediction); call `init_map`.
3. `grow`: `realloc` (question), `grow`; build and deploy; repeating Grow
   from 20 KB, +10 KB a press, until refused with "out of memory".
4. `zero-copy`: `BigMap` (question), its three account structs, the three
   instructions; build and deploy; create; Grow to 40 KB (`until`); Paint
   "gm" at 35,000. Goal: `paint` logs `Painted \d+ bytes at \d+`.

Every write names its `ref`, as the script does: the program repeats `}`,
`bump,` and `pub signer` in every accounts struct.
