# 7.1 Retire the lesson layer (HOO-2365)

Design D7: Hello Anchor opens in the conversation (`tutorial.json`,
`openOnIntro`), so the band, rail, reader and phone bar of
`client-v2-lessons` go, with `PgLesson`'s key and `migrate.ts`. The ticket
also carries the "no stage before Start" bug (+0.25 d).

## Success metric

Opening any tutorial shows either the conversation or upstream's pages, never
the band. Check: a manual sweep of the 16 tutorials before 10 Nov.

## Design gaps

1. The stepper's brand dot on the lesson step's target stage goes with the
   lesson: nothing else sets a target. Decided: the stepper shows no target.
2. Hello Anchor on a window under 1024 px keeps today's layout (D7); without
   the band it would lose the lesson. Decided (Design alignment, 3 Nov): the
   conversation takes the assistant's column there (the chat sheet at 900
   px), until `tutorial-phone` (HOO-2359) lands. A learner at 768 to 1023 px
   lands on the editor and opens the chat from the header's chat button to
   see the lesson; it does not open itself, which needs the layout shell
   (HOO-2548, HOO-2359). Interim: per DES-160 (Almaz, 3 Nov) 768 to 1023 px
   becomes the phone screens at tablet width (HOO-2359), and 1024 to 1151
   px the desktop layout (HOO-2548).
3. Before Start, the frame shows its stage tabs (the prototype has none).
   Decided: hidden until Start; whether they come back after Start is the
   frame ticket's (HOO-2548).
