# Lumberjack as a Conversation Tutorial

**Goal:** Lumberjack, the Intermediate tutorial for Breakpoint, runs on the
conversation flow: a player account at a PDA, a custom error when the energy
runs out, and energy that refills over time with no transaction (HOO-2362).

**Spec:** `tutorial-definition`, written from a prototype script; task 6.3.

## Source

Almaz's lesson `design-system/src/prototype/tutorial-v2/lessons/lumberjack.ts`
on `almaz/mobile-tutorial-prototype` at `f7d42bf2e` (2 Nov), the commit the
design audit read. The first version, `76f5db690` (28 Oct), still ends its
headlines with a period; `d90ed3222` (31 Oct) took them off, as Slava's
comment on the ticket asks. `SPEC.md` beside it holds no Lumberjack copy.

The lesson builds `lib.rs` from pieces in code, so the definition is not
copied by hand: the lesson module is loaded with `tsx`, dumped to JSON, and
converted field by field along `AUTHORING.md`'s "From a prototype script".
The copy is carried over word for word.

## Decisions

- **Each write keeps the prototype's `ref`.** `AUTHORING.md` drops a
  prototype write's `ref`, but HOO-2504 added `ref` to place writes the way
  the prototype does, and every block here sits once in its `ref`, which the
  check confirms. Step 1's writes share the failing build as `ref`, so the
  explanation's "line 23" is the line the learner sees.
- **`failsWith` is the real build server's line**, "requires a non-optional
  system_program field to exist in the account validation struct". The
  prototype mocks "the init constraint requires…"; the compiler also points
  at line 29 (`pub player`), where the explanation quotes line 23 (`init,`),
  as the prototype has it.
- **Changed from the prototype, and why:**
  - the client's `initPlayer()` names `.accounts({ player: playerPda })`:
    Playground's Anchor 0.29 client fills the signer and the System Program,
    not a PDA whose seeds the build server's IDL leaves out ("Invalid
    arguments: player not provided"); the prototype mocks its runs;
  - the two upgrade cards read `{program}` where the prototype has its own
    program id, as Hello Anchor's `s4.ship`;
  - the finish line drops the live wood count: "Your player's wood is on
    devnet, …";
  - an act's `context` drops its backticks: the card draws it as plain text.
- **Goals:** step 1 `build` with `init_player`; step 2 `call` of
  `init_player` (it prints nothing, so no `log`); step 3 `refused`
  `chop_tree` with `NotEnoughEnergy` (6000) on a repeating `Chop` card
  ("Chop until out of energy", record "Chopped"); step 4 `call` of
  `chop_tree` with `You chopped a tree`. Steps 2 to 4 draw the player
  account; step 4's has `refill` (30 s from `lastLogin`), so its card shows
  the wait.
- **The upstream Markdown Lumberjack leaves the list.** A loaded definition
  replaces the other tutorial with its slug (`listTutorials`, from Zero
  Copy's #391), so `/tutorials` lists one Lumberjack.
- **The deploy and RPC decisions are in the change
  [`deploy-rpc-pacing`](../../deploy-rpc-pacing/proposal.md).** Deploying
  Lumberjack through a platform RPC needed paced buffer writes under a
  per-cluster send cap, a load that fails after rounds in which no chunk
  lands, buffer reads through a read-buffer RPC, a Deploy stage that closes
  its buffer without asking, Stop for builds only, a typed-in public URL kept
  as a custom RPC, and faucet links with the wallet filled in. Those are
  requirements of the deploy, Stop, custom RPC, `tutorial-runs` and
  `client-run` specs rather than of this task, so that change holds them and
  their tasks.
- **Docs:** the definition lists Solana's PDA page, Anchor's custom errors
  page and the sysvar section of Solana's account types page, with copies
  under `lumberjack/docs/`.
- **Left for their own tickets:** a step 2 that the player account already
  passed cannot pass again (Start over keeps the program id, and the client
  then sends nothing; the format has no goal for it), the Explorer toasts
  over the chat after repeated calls, the squeezed call card with an account
  and no repeat, and no Try again after a failed run or call. Filed on the
  tickets that own them.
