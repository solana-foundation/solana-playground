> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** An author outside the team (Cat McGee) drafts a conversation tutorial with any LLM from `AUTHORING.md` and `tutorial.schema.json`, and checks it with `yarn tutorial:check`.

**Architecture:** No code. Try the recipe on two LLMs that see only the two files, fix in `AUTHORING.md` what made them stumble, and add a short human how-to beside it.

**Spec:** HOO-2647 (Linear) and task 1.3 in `../tasks.md`.
